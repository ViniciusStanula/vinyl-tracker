#
# Other AI training crawlers (meta-externalagent, ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Bytespider)
# are deliberately NOT blocked, despite being 58% of bot traffic in bot_hits
# and a real share of the ISR/transfer bill. Presence in training data is what
# makes a model name this site unprompted, and that visibility is worth more
# here than the hosting it costs. Revisit only if the bill forces it.
